# Sample audio features for testing

## Pitch Variation
- Value: 0.85
- Description: Measures variation in pitch over time; synthetic voices often have less variation

## Voice Quality
- Value: 0.92
- Description: Indicates clarity and naturalness of the voice; lower values may indicate synthetic audio

## Speaking Rate
- Value: 1.15
- Description: Ratio of actual speaking speed to normal rate; deviations can suggest AI generation
